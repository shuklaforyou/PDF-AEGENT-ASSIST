# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people. In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Using welcoming and inclusive language** (avoid gendered language, consider accessibility)
- **Being constructive** with criticism and feedback
- **Focusing on what is best** for the community and project
- **Showing empathy** towards other community members
- **Taking responsibility** and apologizing when wrong

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or intimidation of any kind
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Violence or threats of violence
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Advocating for, or encouraging any of the above behaviors

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces managed by the PDF Agent Assist project, including:

- GitHub repositories (issues, PRs, discussions)
- Code comments and documentation
- Project-related events and communications
- Social media accounts associated with the project

This Code of Conduct also applies outside project spaces if conduct is associated with the project or its community and may negatively affect the community.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported to the project maintainers at [maintainer-email@example.com](mailto:maintainer-email@example.com).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

## Consequences

Those who violate this Code of Conduct will face consequences proportionate to the severity of the offense, including:

1. **Verbal warning** — First-time minor violations
2. **Temporary ban** — Repeated violations or serious conduct
3. **Permanent ban** — Severe or repeated harassment

Project maintainers will explain the reason for any action taken.

## Appeal Process

If you believe you have been falsely accused or unfairly sanctioned, you may appeal to [appeal-contact@example.com](mailto:appeal-contact@example.com) with:
- Explanation of the situation
- Why you believe the action was unfair
- Any supporting evidence

Appeals will be reviewed by a different team member and responded to within 10 business days.

## Community Values

### Inclusivity

We welcome and encourage participation from people of all backgrounds and identities. PDF Agent Assist is stronger and better because of the diversity of our community.

### Transparency

We believe in open communication. Decisions affecting the community should be transparent, and community members should have a voice in important decisions.

### Collaboration

We're stronger together. We value collaboration, mentoring, and helping each other grow.

### Respect

We respect each other's time, perspectives, and boundaries. Disagreement is fine; disrespect is not.
